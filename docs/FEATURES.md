# ✨ Features

Pages are grouped into six sections in the top dock: **Home**, **Live**, **Career**, **Squad**, **Arsenal** and **Playbook**. Discord, What's new, Feedback and Settings sit on the right of the dock, and **Ctrl+K** opens a quick search that jumps to any page.

## 🏠 Home
Your account at a glance: rank and RR, peak rank, wallet, daily offers with their reset timer, battle pass progress, and online friends.

## 🔴 Live

### 🎯 Live Match
Two tabs that follow the game: **Lobby & Party** in menus, **Live Match** from agent select on.

**Lobby & Party**
- Your party with ranks, the leader's crown and a Ready / Standby toggle.
- Invite friends by Riot ID, or join a party with an invite code.
- Pick a mode, start or cancel matchmaking, open or close your party, and generate an invite code. Mode, matchmaking, access and codes are leader-only.
- During a match these lock; inviting still works.

**Agent select**
- Your team's agents, lock state, and every teammate's rank and peak. Enemies aren't shown until the match starts.
- **Change skins** opens your loadout editor in a side panel. Changes are equipped in game right away.

**In match**
- Player cards with card art, current-act rank and RR, peak rank, and Vandal / Phantom skins. Your party is marked with a colored bar, and premade parties on both teams are tagged **Duo**, **Trio**, **4-stack** or **5-stack** in matching colors.
- **Scout** (any player) opens a side panel: current rank and career peak, recent performance (win rate, KDA, combat score, headshot %, KAST, damage per round, first bloods), rank history by act, top agents, and the last 10 competitive matches with RR changes.
- **👁 Skins** (any player) shows their card and title, sprays, and every weapon skin with its gun buddy.
- Deathmatch splits everyone across both columns.
- An always-on-top **overlay** with both rosters, ranks, RR, peaks and team average rank. Show or hide it with a global hotkey (default `Ctrl+Shift+O`), lock it so clicks pass through to the game (`Ctrl+Shift+L`), or let it open automatically during matches. It never steals focus from the game. Works over VALORANT in Windowed fullscreen.

**Streamer mode:** players who hide their name are shown only by their agent. Their name, Riot ID, account level, card and title never appear, in rows or panels. The only exceptions are you and friends in your own party.

## 📊 Career

### 📈 Progression
Battle pass and agent contract progress, account level, and your competitive RR history.

### 🕘 Matches
Your recent matches with scores, K/D/A, ACS, ADR, headshot % and the RR you gained or lost. Open any match for the full scoreboard and round-by-round breakdown. Every player is named, with a link to their profile and notes, except streamer-mode players in your current match, who show only their agent.

### 🤝 Encounters
Friends and party members you played with in your last 20 matches, sorted by how often. Other players stay hidden.

### 🏆 Leaderboard
The competitive leaderboard for your region and the current act, with friends highlighted.

## 👥 Squad

### 👥 Friends
Online friends with what they're doing in VALORANT, incoming and outgoing friend requests, your current party, and direct-message chat.

### 👤 Feed
A timeline of your friends list: who added you, who removed you and who changed their name. Spectre checks every couple of minutes while it's open and catches anything that changed while it was closed. Filter by type, search by name, copy a Riot ID, or scan right away. Switching Riot accounts never logs fake changes.

### 📝 Notes
A notebook saved on your PC. Make sections (say, one per map), give each a color and icon, and keep in each:
- **Notes:** lineups, setups and strats (up to 3,000 characters, saved as you type)
- **Crosshairs:** named crosshair codes with one-click copy
- **Links:** guides and lineup videos that open in your browser
- **Chat snippets:** messages you send often, with one-click copy

Notes on players still work: add one from Live Match, Friends or a scoreboard, and it shows next to that player in Live Match.

### 🔍 Lookup
Look up any player by Riot ID (`Name#Tag`): current and peak rank, act-by-act history and recent competitive form.

## 🎒 Arsenal

### 🎒 Loadout
Your equipped weapon skins, sprays, buddies, card and title, plus saved presets. Pick a weapon, choose a skin, level, variant and buddy, and press **Apply** to equip it right away. Only items you own are listed.

### 🛒 Store
Featured bundles, daily offers, accessories and the Night Market when it's live, with prices and time remaining.

### 🔫 Weapons
Every weapon with its stats and damage falloff by range, plus every skin with video previews, levels and variants.

## 🗺️ Playbook

A whiteboard for planning rounds. Pick a map, place agents and abilities, and draw arrows and zones. Strategies are grouped into playbooks and saved locally.

## ⚙️ Dock extras

### 🎮 Discord
Discord Rich Presence through Spectre's own Discord app: one switch to turn it on, no setup.
- **Match details:** game mode, map art, agent (optionally as the main image), rank with RR, peak rank.
- **Session details:** account level, party size or "Solo", round score, elapsed time, and whether to stay on in the lobby.
- **Profile preview** with Live and sample scenarios (Competitive, Deathmatch, Team DM, Agent Select, Lobby, In Queue) using your real rank.

### 📰 What's new
Two tabs: **Spectre** release notes straight from GitHub, and the latest official **VALORANT** patch notes.

### 💡 Feedback
Send ideas, bug reports or anything else straight to the developer by email. Only your message, an optional contact and your Spectre version are sent.

### ⬆️ Updates
Spectre checks for new releases on launch and every few hours. When one is out, it offers a one-click update that downloads and verifies the installer, installs it with just a progress bar and restarts. Dev builds and copies run without the installer get a link to the new download instead. Check any time from **Settings → About**.

### 🎨 Settings
Eighteen glass themes (Midnight, Onyx, Ember, Glacier, Verdant, Amethyst, Gilded, Aurora, Sakura, Abyss, Sunset, the light Pearl, Frutiger Aero, Vaporwave, Y2K Chrome, Lo-fi, Minty Jamun and Musashi), reduce motion, the start page, launch at login (on by default, starts in the tray), close to tray (on by default), minimize to tray, and overlay hotkeys, click-through, auto-show, opacity and size.
