Know who's in your match before round one.

Spectre is a free, open-source companion for VALORANT on Windows. It shows ranks, peaks and skins for all ten players, scouts anyone in one click and runs your party and store, through the Riot Client you're already signed in to.

Download for WindowsLatest release on GitHub View the source

Free and open source. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Never asks for your Riot password.

Ranks and skins for all ten players

Live Match fills in during agent select and follows the game to the last round. Click any name to scout that player.

Scout anyone in one click

Rank history by act, win rate, KDA, headshot % and their last 10 competitive games. Open it from any name in Live Match, or search a Riot ID.

Overlay hidden. Press the keys again.

Ranks on top of the game

Ctrl Shift O shows it, Ctrl Shift L lets clicks pass through. It never steals focus. The shortcut works in the app; on this page the button shows you.

Everything around the match

Six sections in one top dock, the same as in the app.

Home

Your rank and RR, wallet, battle pass progress, today's store and who's online, on the first screen you see.

Live

Both teams from agent select to the last round: rank, RR, peak, skins and your notes. Lobby and Party sits one tab over.

Career

Recent matches with score, K/D/A, ACS, ADR and headshot %. Open any game for the full scoreboard and every round, then check your RR history and the leaderboard.

Squad

See what friends are playing, chat, invite by Riot ID or party code, and ready up from your desktop without opening the game.

Arsenal

Daily offers, bundles and the Night Market with timers. Equip skins, variants and buddies you own, even from agent select.

Playbook

Draw rounds on every map with agents, abilities and paths, then save them to run with your team.

Discord presence: mode, map, score and rank on your profile.

Questions

Can I get banned for using Spectre?

Spectre isn't a cheat. It never reads or changes the game, doesn't touch Vanguard, and talks to Riot the same way the VALORANT client does. It's still an unofficial, fan-made tool, so use it at your own risk.

Spectre says it's waiting for the Riot Client

Open the Riot Client and sign in. Spectre connects on its own within a few seconds.

Live Match is empty

It fills in once agent select starts. Before that, the Squad section shows your party.

How do I update?

Spectre checks for new versions on launch and offers a one-click update. You can also check any time from Settings, About, Check for updates.

How do I uninstall?

Open Windows Settings, then Apps, Installed apps, Spectre. To remove saved data too, delete %APPDATA%\Spectre.
