Live Match

Ranks and skins for all ten players

Live Match fills in during agent select and follows the game until the last round.

  • Current-act rank, RR and peak rank for everyone
  • Vandal and Phantom skins on every card
  • Your party marked with a coloured bar
  • Streamer-mode players shown by agent only

Scout any player in one click

Rank history by act, win rate, KDA, headshot % and their last 10 competitive games.

Keeps ranks on top of the game

Ctrl Shift O shows the overlay, Ctrl Shift L lets clicks pass through. It never steals focus.

Everything around the match

Arsenal: store and loadout

Daily offers, bundles and the Night Market with timers. Equip skins, variants and buddies you own, even from agent select.

Playbook for your strats

Midnight theme

Not a cheat, no accounts, no tracking

Spectre is a regular desktop app that talks to the Riot Client on your PC.

Never touches the game

  • No reading or changing game memory or files, and nothing injected
  • No contact with Vanguard
  • Talks to Riot the way the VALORANT client does, with your own session
  • Only changes things when you ask, like equipping a skin

Private by design

  • No Riot password: it uses the Riot Client you're already signed in to
  • No servers of its own, no analytics, no telemetry
  • Streamer-mode players in your match are never named (only you and friends in your party are shown)
  • Settings and notes stay on your PC in %APPDATA%\Spectre

Questions and answers

Can I get banned for using Spectre?

Spectre isn't a cheat. It never reads or changes the game, doesn't touch Vanguard, and talks to Riot the same way the VALORANT client does. It's an unofficial, fan-made tool that Riot hasn't reviewed or approved, so nobody outside Riot can promise how they treat third-party apps. The Experimental tools in Settings change how your profile looks to others; they're off by default, so leave them off if you want to play it safe.

Is Spectre made by Riot?

No. Spectre is an independent, open-source project. It isn't made, endorsed or supported by Riot Games, and it's provided as is, without any warranty. All of its code is on GitHub, so anyone can check what it does. Only download it from this site or the GitHub releases page.

Spectre says it's waiting for the Riot Client

Open the Riot Client and sign in. Spectre connects on its own within a few seconds.

Live Match is empty

It fills in once agent select starts. Before that, the Squad section shows your party.

How do I update?

Spectre checks for new versions on launch and offers a one-click update. You can also check any time from Settings, About, Check for updates.

How do I uninstall?

Open Windows Settings, then Apps, Installed apps, Spectre. To remove saved data too, delete %APPDATA%\Spectre.
