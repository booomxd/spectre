Features

Everything VALORANT doesn't show you

Spectre talks to the Riot Client on your PC, so there's nothing to sign in to and nothing to set up.

Live Match

Every player's current-act rank, RR and peak, with their Vandal and Phantom skins. Click anyone to Scout them: rank history, recent form and their last 10 competitive matches.

Lobby & Party

Invite friends, share codes, pick a mode, ready up and queue without opening the game.

Skins

See anyone's loadout in a match, and change your own skins right from agent select.

Discord presence

Mode, map art, score, rank and party on your profile. One switch, no setup.

Streamer-safe

Players who hide their name in your match stay hidden everywhere in Spectre. No names, IDs or cards.

In-game overlay

Safety & privacy

Not a cheat. No accounts. No tracking.

Never touches the game

  • No reading or changing game memory or files, and nothing injected
  • No contact with Vanguard: it's a regular desktop app
  • Talks to Riot the same way the VALORANT client does, with your own session
  • Only changes things when you ask, like equipping a skin

Private by design

  • No Riot password and no accounts: it uses the Riot Client already signed in
  • No servers of its own, no analytics, no telemetry
  • Streamer-mode players in your match are never named or identified
  • Settings and notes stay on your PC in %APPDATA%\Spectre

FAQ

Questions

Can I get banned for using Spectre?

Spectre isn't a cheat. It never reads or changes the game, doesn't touch Vanguard, and talks to Riot the same way the VALORANT client does. It's still an unofficial, fan-made tool, so use it at your own risk.

Windows says ffmpeg.dll was not found

The portable app was started from inside the zip, or without its other files. Right-click the zip, choose Extract All, and run Spectre.exe from the extracted folder. Or use the installer. If it keeps happening, check your antivirus quarantine.

Spectre says it's waiting for the Riot Client

Open the Riot Client and sign in. Spectre connects on its own within a few seconds.

Live Match is empty

It fills in once agent select starts. Before that, the Lobby & Party tab shows your party.

How do I update?

Spectre checks for new versions on launch and offers a one-click update. You can also check any time from Settings → About → Check for updates. Portable copies get a link to the new download.

How do I uninstall?

Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Spectre. For the portable version, delete its folder. To remove saved data too, delete %APPDATA%\Spectre.
